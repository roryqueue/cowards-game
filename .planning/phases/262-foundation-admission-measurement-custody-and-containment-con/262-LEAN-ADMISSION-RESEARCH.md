## Common Pitfalls

### Reinterpreting history
**What goes wrong:** The new pass is reported as `540/540` or as proof that the old matrix is reproducible.  
**Avoidance:** Keep `historicalFullMatrix` and `leanGate` as separate required objects and test the exact wording/booleans. [VERIFIED: historical terminal/disposition]

### Comparing unstable bytes
**What goes wrong:** Pass roots differ because request IDs or timing fields differ, even when semantics match.  
**Avoidance:** Define and test one explicit normalization projection before execution; strip only pass/invocation identity and wall-clock metadata. [VERIFIED: existing canonical reducer patterns]

### Accidentally creating competitive evidence
**What goes wrong:** Win/loss rows leak into standings or later training inputs.  
**Avoidance:** Label every artifact `fixture_feasibility_only`, omit standings, and add negative import/reference monitors. [VERIFIED: activation contract]

### Resource-policy recursion
**What goes wrong:** A small gate grows another preflight/calibration/review/authorization chain.  
**Avoidance:** One manifest, one command, one receipt, one code review, one validation; at most one whole-gate corrective rerun for a committed defect. [VERIFIED: approved lean intent]
