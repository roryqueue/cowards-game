## Summary

Plan 262-92 failed closed before publication because `authenticateReviewedExecutionClosure` expects `review.correctedSource.commit` or `review.reviewedSource.commit` plus either `review.executionClosure.executionClosureRoot` or top-level `review.executionClosureRoot`. The immutable Plan-97 JSON instead carries the source commit at `execution.sourceCommit` and the installed dependency closure at `execution.installedClosureRoot`; it carries no full `executionClosureRoot`. [VERIFIED: `scripts/run-v1-38-bounded-retry-envelope-v3.ts:201-223`; Plan-97 canonical JSON]

This was a safe integrity stop. Seal-v13, retry-envelope:v3, journal-v3, terminal-v3, and reproduction-v17 are absent; fresh charged and accepted remain `0/0`; no local secret was accessed and no capacity identity was consumed. [VERIFIED: filesystem; Plan-97 authority projection; assigned failure facts]

Do not make the producer treat `execution.installedClosureRoot` as the full execution root. The full root also binds the authenticated Git executable/isolation/object root, exact source commit/tree/parent, checkout manifest, Node/pnpm bytes, native source root, and claim boundary. The installed root is only one member of that body. [VERIFIED: `V138RetryV3ExecutionClosure` and `authenticateV138RetryV3ExecutionClosure`]

The smallest safe correction is a new two-plan additive pair: Plan 262-98 corrects the producer/review contract and its tests without publishing authority; Plan 262-99 independently reviews the exact committed Plan-98 source and publishes a new zero-finding-or-blocked JSON/REVIEW pair. Plan 92 then consumes only a literal-zero Plan-99 pair. Plans 96/97 remain immutable protected history and are never reinterpreted. [PRESCRIPTIVE; pattern VERIFIED: Plans 96/97]

**Primary recommendation:** use a new portable reviewed-execution-closure schema, not a fallback that equates the Plan-97 installed root with the full closure and not an edit to Plan 97. Plan 99 should root the portable fields that can match between its detached checkout and the later authoritative checkout; Plan 92's producer should independently derive the current full environment-bound root, validate every portable field against Plan 99, and require the same current full root before and after derivation/publication. [PRESCRIPTIVE]
