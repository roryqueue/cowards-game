### F-262-111-01 — The reviewed live-v9 closure has no CLI path to readiness or its sole production function

**Severity:** critical  
**Location:** `scripts/run-v1-38-bounded-retry-envelope-v3-live-v9.ts:67-71,1188-1238`; `262-110-PLAN.md:73-82`; `262-111-RESEARCH.md:157-166`

`V138_LIVE_V9_MODES` contains only `--check-source-only`, `--check-prospective-custody`, and `--check-post-run-custody`. `executeV138LiveV9Cli` rejects every other argument and never calls `runV138ReviewedBoundedLiveEnvelopeV9`. Consequently, revised Plan 110's exact pre-effect command `--check-reviewed-live-ready` and sole authorized live command `--run-reviewed-bounded-live-envelope` both fail before authentication or dispatch.

This is not safely repairable in Plan 110 or a later wrapper: changing live-v9 after Plan 112 reviews its source would invalidate the reviewed closure, while a new caller would be executable code outside the closure that supplement-v2 binds. The current unit test explicitly freezes the broken condition by asserting that no production CLI mode exists.

**Required fix:** add the exact readiness and production selectors to this adapter before Plan 112 review. The readiness selector must execute only the full published Plan-112/supplement-v2 pre-effect authentication. The production selector must call the closed `runV138ReviewedBoundedLiveEnvelopeV9(repoRoot)` exactly once and expose no producer/gate injection. Plan 111 verification must continue to avoid invoking the production selector; absence of a verification invocation does not require absence of the reviewed selector.

### F-262-111-02 — Post-run custody necessarily rejects canonical producer effects

**Severity:** critical  
**Location:** `scripts/run-v1-38-bounded-retry-envelope-v3-live-v9.ts:966-1000,1075-1080,1161-1181`

`ALWAYS_FORBIDDEN_DESTINATIONS` includes the v3 journal, journal lock, private receipt directory, terminal, and reproduction. `authenticateV138LiveV9Base` always checks that full list, including when `authenticateV138LiveV9FutureCustody(repoRoot, true)` is called from the production function's `finally` block and by `--check-post-run-custody`.

Those are the canonical destinations the unchanged historical producer owns. Any started producer route creates at least the private directory and journal and eventually a terminal; a successful reproduction may also create reproduction-v17. Therefore the required finally-equivalent custody check reports `V138_LIVE_V9_FORBIDDEN_DESTINATION_PRESENT` after legitimate producer work. On an otherwise successful producer outcome the adapter converts success into custody failure; on a producer rejection it reports an aggregate error containing an artificial custody failure. The advertised post-run check cannot validate a completed run.

**Required fix:** split pre-effect absence checks from post-effect custody checks. Before dispatch, require every live destination absent. After dispatch, continue to require supplement-v1 and all unauthorized downstream destinations absent, but authenticate the producer-owned journal/private/terminal/reproduction according to the frozen v3 producer contract rather than forbidding them. Retain source, corrected-chain, Plan-112, supplement-v2, pair, Plan-93, and protected-history no-rewrite checks before and after, and preserve lone versus simultaneous errors exactly.
