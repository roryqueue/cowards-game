## Tasks

1. Add focused checker proof tooling.

   - Rerun checker boundary monitors.
   - Confirm TinyGo remains spike-only/hidden.
   - Confirm TypeScript/Python claims remain provenance-only and Rust/Zig remain immutable WASM/WASI Preview 1 artifact-backed.
   - Document unresolved TypeScript Go account-save gap if still present and non-blocking.

4. Write final evidence and audit artifacts.
   - Produce `.planning/artifacts/v1.34-workshop-checker-proof.md`.
   - Produce Phase 242 summary and verification.
   - Update requirement traceability and state for milestone audit readiness.
