## Implementation Decisions

### One-way materialization gate and unchanged authority
- **D-01:** No formation constructor, namespace, executable state, manifest, candidate, prompt, cache, trace, replay, or result may be created until the exact Phase 266 root and its pre-formation absence receipt verify.
- **D-02:** Materialize exactly three lab profiles: `current-edge`, `full-inward`, and `edge-anchored-bracket`. All three—including current-edge—are private experimental evidence and use the same lab-only schemas and stores.
- **D-03:** Construct each arm by first deriving the exact canonical current initial state from the admitted production constructor, deep-cloning it inside the private lab, and replacing only the sixteen Soldier position fields according to the frozen mapping. Do not add a production `initialStateProfile`, alternate rules tuple, arena variant, or profile-specific transition loop.
- **D-04:** From transition one onward, every arm advances only through the exact admitted `MATCH_KERNEL`. No copied resolver, alternate scheduler, formation-specific legality, or changed event/observation rule is allowed.
- **D-05:** Top Soldiers retain `DOWN`; bottom Soldiers retain `UP`. Soldier IDs, owner, status, memory, reversal history, Strategy bindings, initiative, activation counts, bounds, terrain, scoring, rules/runtime/Chronicle versions, and every other field remain unchanged.

### Frozen x-preserving Soldier mapping
- **D-06:** Soldier identity is permanently tied to current x-order: Soldier IDs 1–8 map to x coordinates 2–9 respectively for both sides. No profile may reorder identities to alter tie-breaks, memory, objectives, or source-observed ordering.

Paired diffs permit only the lab profile identity in its private envelope and the sixteen declared position fields.
- **D-09:** For each side, bracket fixtures must prove: four rear-wing behind squares are off-board; the four rear-center squares are empty and initially sealed; all eight directly inward squares are empty; exactly four Soldiers remain on the first-Contraction boundary; and protection decays through ordinary movement rather than disabling Backstab.

### Current control equivalence and private evidence
- **D-10:** `current-edge` must match canonical current construction field-for-field before execution and match canonical transition, final-state, event, observation, StrategyMemory, and SoldierMemory roots across the fixed corpus. Any mismatch blocks all three arms.
- **D-11:** Use a distinct versioned private lab-trace schema for reconstruction and replay review. It may reuse pure internal replay/reconstruction helpers, but it is not a Chronicle, cannot pass Chronicle admission, and cannot become a product replay.
- **D-12:** Lab profile identity belongs to the private lab envelope/manifest and must not widen canonical Match, Strategy, arena, Chronicle, persistence, runtime-service, or public DTO schemas.
- **D-13:** Current-edge formation-control candidates created later are lab-only and product-ineligible, just like inward and bracket candidates. Only exact pre-formation current-league source hashes listed by Phase 266 may cross the later promotion bridge.

### Production-unreachability proof
- **D-14:** Canonical Strategy/arena registration, account save, production initial-state construction, runtime-service Match requests, Go creation/jobs, canonical persistence, and evidence-tuple admission must reject every experimental profile and artifact.
- **D-15:** Counted and exhibition scheduling, Chronicle admission, MatchSet scoring, standings, result/replay/discovery DTOs and pages, public/default output, production images, generated contracts, and deployment manifests must also reject or omit every experimental profile and artifact.
- **D-16:** Prove those denials with static dependency/import checks, strict-schema negatives, positive bypass probes at every boundary, mutation tests that demonstrate guards are effective, selector and generated-artifact drift checks, exact source-hash joins, public/privacy scans, and a complete denial matrix.
- **D-17:** Rollback proof removes/disables the lab path and reproduces unchanged canonical current construction, kernel, Chronicle, replay, service, persistence, scheduling, standings, public, image, and deployment hashes. Run the proof against a synthetic “passing bracket” disposition so a favorable result cannot activate a latent production path.
- **D-18:** A passing bracket remains `production_authorized: false`. Phase 267 creates no public preview, toggle, registration record, counted/exhibition entry, canonical evidence, or migration path.

### Fixed-variable exclusions
- **D-19:** The experiment keeps Cycle cap 12, all nine Actions, MOVE/facing/reversal, collision/push, Backstab geometry and timing, activation counts, Contraction, scoring/outcomes, active semantic arenas, runtime/source/memory/objective/output limits, and every other rule fixed.
- **D-20:** Cycle-cap reduction, facing-only MOVE, reversal-history removal, attacker-facing or Advance-causal Backstab, scan-timing changes, new arenas, runtime changes, and combined interaction profiles are unavailable here and require separately approved later experiments.

### the agent's Discretion
- After the required Phase 267 spike, planning may choose the exact private position-only state-entry seam and lab-trace representation, provided construction starts from the canonical state, only the frozen positions change, the private profile identity never enters canonical schemas, and transition one onward uses `MATCH_KERNEL`.
- Private module/file layout, trace compression, review tooling, and denial-test decomposition are flexible only if all three arms share the same path and the exhaustive reachability/rollback contract remains machine-checkable.
- Stable internal profile schema/reason-code names are flexible; the three semantic profiles, exact coordinate mapping, evidence class, and production denials are not.
