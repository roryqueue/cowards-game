This phase accepts only the three valid Phase-268 branch freezes, performs one common content-addressed holdout batch through the separately controlled evaluator, applies every frozen causal and integrity gate without compensation, emits an immutable pass or rejection packet with no production authority, and certifies every eligible exact pre-formation current-league finalist through its ordinary native product path. <decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Carry-forward trust charter
- **D-01:** Holdout requests, receipts, results, causal reports, certification evidence, and decision packets are immutable, content-addressed, and have no mutable `latest` identity. Missing, stale, mismatched, incomplete, contaminated, or unresolvable inputs fail closed.
- **D-02:** The exact selected canonical `MATCH_KERNEL` remains the sole transition authority and every hostile Strategy executes through the supervised runtime boundary. No copied rules, evaluator-specific transition loop, web/API/Go Strategy execution, or hidden fallback is permitted.
- **D-03:** Every attempt, continuation, failure, rejection, and unused allocation remains charged evidence. Process-valid current-rules failure or bracket rejection is an honest result; a process, custody, equality, information, or reproducibility failure invalidates the sealed claim.
- **D-04:** All formation-arm evidence, including current-edge, remains private lab evidence and unreachable from registration, persistence, scheduling, Chronicle, standings, replay, selectors, defaults, and public output.
- **D-05:** Cycle cap, MOVE/reversal, Backstab geometry/timing, activation counts, arenas, runtime, and every other rule remain fixed. No result can authorize a rule change or claim exact exploitability, Nash equilibrium, optimality, permanent balance, or a solved game.

### One logical sealed batch
- **D-06:** Before accepting a request, the custodian verifies the original commitment; all three exact branch-freeze roots; their preselected finalist hash sets; threshold and analysis roots; equal-compute receipts; the common profile-agnostic opponent/schedule root; evaluator, schema, kernel, and runtime identities; and the frozen permitted metric projection.
- **D-07:** All three profiles are evaluated in one logical content-addressed batch against the same common arena, opponent, and schedule holdout. The receipt binds the exact request root, commitment verification, three freeze roots, result roots, bounded release, and literal `openOrdinal: 1`.
- **D-08:** A transport interruption may continue only as an idempotent continuation of the byte-identical request and request root, against unchanged evaluator state, before any partial or aggregate result is released. It is the same logical batch, not a second query.
- **D-09:** Partial disclosure, an altered continuation, uncertain evaluator state, premature access, commitment or freeze mismatch, unauthorized query, or any result release before completion is contamination. The sealed claim is invalidated and preserved as such; it is never repaired by a substitute holdout or silent rerun.
- **D-10:** After the open begins, no source, candidate, population, finalist, threshold, selection rule, metric, classifier, interpretation, prompt, or analysis code may change. The evaluator cannot nominate a different finalist after seeing results, and no second diagnostic query is allowed.
- **D-11:** Side, initial initiative, horizontal symmetry, opaque player and Soldier ids, Soldier/source ordering, deterministic repeat, and held-out-opponent invariance tests run identically for all profiles.
- **D-12:** Raw holdout identities/preimages, evaluator state, opponent source, Strategy source or artifacts, memories, objectives, credentials, host details, security internals, and raw diagnostics stay in separately controlled private evidence. Default or public-safe projection contains only approved aggregates and opaque roots.

### Causal decision and hard gates
- **D-13:** The causal report uses the exact classifiers, denominators, thresholds, replication treatment, and non-compensating logic frozen before results. It reports opening clusters and normalized entropy, viable openings, forced evacuation, first-Contraction reserves, first Awareness/contact/push/Backstab/STONE/decisive timing, Backstab and push/block causes, no-Advance STONE/cleanup, center/wing/convoy/turtle behavior, draws, Match length, Contractions, ACTIVE survival, response gaps, pure worst cases, and best-response graphs.
- **D-14:** Every mandatory gate is independent and non-compensating. Attractive average Set score, a composite score, or strength on another metric cannot override scripted-opening convergence, collapsed opening diversity, convoy/reserve-hoarding/persistent-turtle/STONE-shield behavior, materially lower or later interaction, inactivity, draws, pathological blocking, worse oracle-relative response gap, pure-worst-case failure, invariance failure, unequal compute, information failure, or a frozen causal confounder.
- **D-15:** A passing bracket produces a linked private causal bundle and a content-addressed privacy-safe decision packet with exact opaque roots and approved aggregates, uncertainty and oracle/budget limits, unchanged-rule and equality proof, the bracket's composite-mechanism attribution limitation, and separate follow-up questions. Its closed status fields are `eligible_for_later_consideration` and `production_authorized: false`.
- **D-16:** A bracket rejection or process-valid current-rules empirical failure receives an equally durable report. Pass, rejection, and current-rules failure remain distinct from process failure and none changes canonical rules, registration, persistence, scheduling, standings, replay semantics, selectors, defaults, or public behavior.

### Exact-source current-only certification
- **D-17:** The promotion bridge accepts only an exact source hash from the eligible pre-formation current-league finalist list frozen in Phase 266. Phase-268 current-edge control candidates are lab evidence and are ineligible alongside inward and bracket candidates.
- **D-18:** Every frozen eligible current finalist must receive the contracted certification attempt; no finalist may be dropped after formation or holdout results are known. An empty eligible list records `no_certifiable_current_finalist` and makes no promotion or successful-certification claim.
- **D-19:** Certification re-admits the exact immutable source through the ordinary Strategy Revision path on its declared supported language/provider/runtime lane. Each lane must be proved independently with no translation, inferred coverage, omitted-lane pass, substitute provider, or fallback.
- **D-20:** Each candidate passes exact-source execution, deterministic repeat, resource and source limits, containment, player/system failure ownership, runtime-service identity, and immutable revision proof on its native lane.
- **D-21:** Each candidate then produces a version-strict semantically valid canonical Chronicle whose reconstruction, ordered transition, terminal, and trace roots match canonical execution.
- **D-22:** Each candidate passes Go-owned completion, canonical persistence, MatchSet scoring/status, standings/governance recomputation, idempotency, retry, rollback, immutable history, privacy-safe result/replay/standing projection, and end-to-end proof.
- **D-23:** Certification is evidence, not activation. It cannot itself change counted eligibility, publication, production defaults, selectors, governance policy, or existing authority, and no experimental trace, telemetry, manifest, source, or decision artifact may traverse the certification path.

### the agent's Discretion
Only after the sacrificial synthetic one-open drill succeeds, the agent may choose strict receipt and packet schema names, internal evaluator transaction/checkpoint mechanics, idempotency-key representation, private-bundle layout, safe aggregate field layout, report rendering, and certification-suite decomposition. These choices may not permit a second logical query, partial feedback, post-open tuning, altered hard gates, selective finalist certification, cross-lane inference, wider public output, or production authorization. Uncertain evaluator state is contamination, not an implementation-recovery choice.

</decisions>

## Specific Ideas

- Model the holdout as one logical batch, so an idempotent byte-identical continuation before disclosure is recoverable but any partial disclosure or uncertain state is contamination.
- Apply hard gates literally and separately; do not invent a composite mechanism that can rescue the bracket.
- Certify the complete frozen eligible pre-formation set, preventing post-result selection from becoming another hidden degree of freedom.
- Publish only safe aggregates and commitments while retaining the full decision packet privately.
