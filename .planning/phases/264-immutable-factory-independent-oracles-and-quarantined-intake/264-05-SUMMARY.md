## Delivered

1. **Six-dimensional evidence.** Source structure is parsed rather than accepted as a supplied hash. Retained proposal/parent lineage and locked dependency bytes are checked against their graphs. Legal decisions, Chronicle behavior and matchup responses bind issued supervision. Cosmetic names, arbitrary roots, copied receipts and invented provenance cannot establish independence. Missing evidence and unfrozen cutoffs stay unresolved.
2. **Complete private ingestion and calibration path.** Tactical and teacher emitters are called as data-only producers; model bundles retain their full immutable companion and are re-admitted on reload. Accepted intake reopens from its original retained charge instead of submitting twice. Preparation requires exact new policy/protocol/allocation/ingestion/workload bindings. The selected runtime rejects unsupported native lanes, identity drift and benchmark/synthetic transport escape options.
3. **Declared workload and result retention.** Each one-phase workload pins canonical arena, seed, side, initiative, fixed mechanics opponent identity, graph references and resource limits. Every execution has a separate durable start and terminal. Only two distinct, same-candidate receipts with matching graphs/conditions/budgets and opposite predeclared initiative form a pair. Pair existence does not assert measured correlation. Canonical WIN/DRAW/failure is projected from the actual result; missing outcomes are never invented as draws. Pairing/fingerprinting happens after terminal retention and never rewrites an attempt.
4. **Recoverable full evidence.** Complete private receipt, execution, transition, accounting and legal-trace records stream into ordered262144-byte chunks. Aggregate evidence above8MiB and individual larger records survive storage/reopen; all commitments and explicit read bounds are checked. Reopen grants data access, not renewed execution authority.
5. **Concrete development comparisons.** Six hand-authored paired projection cases cover semantic rewrites, shared selectors, symmetry/opaque IDs, near-identical behavior, latent divergence and expected false positives. The evaluator derives all six projections and actual agreement counts; the report accepts issued observations only. These are comparison-mechanics fixtures, not supervised outcomes or empirical source-correspondence proof.
6. **Recursive separation and evaluation checks.** The existing lexical AST graph is shared by both monitors, with a fixed canonical-core inventory and narrow packet-only emitter interface. Root `.strategy-lab` evidence is excluded from source scanning; public copies still fail. An explicit16-fixture4/4/3/3/2 reference index and a five-minute source-only step in existing CI enforce the planned evaluation tests without adding a package, service or empirical selector.
