## Recommendation in plain English

Run one private, single-operator league with automated and model-generated
challengers, no recruited human or external entrants, and no automatic retry.
Use one seed and four rounds. Give nine development challengers a chance to
enter the population, then evaluate against two separately frozen opponents.
Keep the existing scientific gates: unsuccessful or correlated challengers must
remain unsuccessful, and a genuine no-finalist result is acceptable.

Withdrawn draft hard stop: **96 hours, 160 GiB of new evidence, and five Sol authoring
calls totaling at most 240,000 tokens**. These are stop limits, not a completion
estimate. The earlier measured run does not establish Phase 265 throughput or
per-Match storage. A resource/integrity failure would remain incomplete; it would
not finish the phase or unlock formation work. If that risk or resource footprint
is unacceptable, revise the study scope before running rather than shrinking
coverage or weakening gates after seeing results.
