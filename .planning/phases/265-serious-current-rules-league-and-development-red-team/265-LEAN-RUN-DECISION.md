## Why this is a recommendation, not a capacity guarantee

The largest authenticated old trace stores in12.18MB/190artifacts. Its417
recorded invocation wrappers add3.65MB of payload and1,251graph artifacts before
descriptor/filesystem costs. Illustratively applying that one shape to the
proposed path, and adding two raw execution copies for each response Match,
gives roughly110GiB before remaining overhead. That is not a measured future
upper bound: future Strategies, memory/output sizes and Match lengths differ,
and factory trace/descriptor/filesystem costs are additional. The current host
has about210GiB free; the stop budget deliberately does not promise completion.
Preflight actual free space and process headroom; do not delete existing evidence
or fill the host to force a run. If the safe envelope cannot support dispatch,
report that resource limitation instead of silently changing this proposal.

The conditional preflight must include invocation, execution, factory-supervision,
descriptor/journal and filesystem costs, not execution bytes alone. Its documented
scale estimate must leave at least10GiB and500,000records within the130GiB/
8.8-million ordinary work pool, in addition to the separate failure reserve,
and at least20GiB of projected free filesystem space. Use measured data-only
format costs and explicitly state remaining assumptions; this is not a claim
that every future Match has a proven worst-case size. Live before-dispatch
capacity checks and fail-closed stops remain mandatory. If these margins cannot
be established, do not run; the operator must choose more storage or a different
prospective envelope. No resources may be raised silently.

The alternative is to retain the current twelve-initial contract and select a
larger storage/record envelope and suitable storage location. The withdrawn
