## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Code Automation / deterministic offline empirical-game research

**Description:**
Private researchers run complete immutable empirical-game snapshots and bounded response/red-team loops through existing supervised runtime. Frozen model bundles are data only; no live inference enters a Match. Correctness means complete reproducible evidence and truthful bounded results, not a promised champion.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. Sparse, invalid or system-failed cells must never become inferred scores.
2. Hostile Strategy source must never execute outside the supervised runtime.
3. Frozen budgets, evidence, targets or thresholds must not drift after results are seen.
4. A mixture must not become a deployable Strategy or conceal the absence of a robust pure finalist.
5. Private data, holdout information or formation artifacts must not cross the phase boundary.

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in domain expert knowledge.

**Industry Vertical:** Competitive-game research and empirical game-theory evaluation (private deterministic strategy lab)

**User Population:** Research operators and reviewers assessing immutable Strategy Revisions, bounded response oracles, and current-rules league evidence; no player-facing or production user population.

**Stakes Level:** High — the consequence is validity of a private research conclusion and any finalist decision, not player safety or a production guarantee.

**Output Consequence:** Outputs become private evidence for admitting response candidates, comparing pure Strategies and mixtures, running the development red team, and selecting a qualified robust pure finalist or recording that none was found. Diagnostic mixtures and lab reports cannot become deployable Strategies or rules. Exact eligible current-rules pure finalist source may enter only the later separately verified ordinary promotion path.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

Dimension: Complete, balanced payoff matrix
Good: Every expected entrant pair, side/initiative condition, and unique semantic arena cell appears exactly once with a valid terminal result before reduction.
Bad: A missing, duplicate, aliased, invalid, or system-failed cell becomes a draw, zero, or inferred loss; Smoke/Open Field are counted as distinct geometries.
Stakes: Critical
Source: Phase-265 D-07–D-09 and verified current-league policy.

Dimension: Correlation and diversity
Good: Fingerprints, clone decisions, semantic arena identity, and source/order probes demonstrate genuinely distinct response behavior.
Bad: Correlated copies, renamed duplicates, or one narrow exploit are presented as portfolio diversity; clone rejections disappear.
Stakes: High
Source: Phase-265 D-08, D-16, D-18.

Dimension: Exact, bounded solver
Good: One complete immutable snapshot yields byte-identical output across permutation, repeat, worker, shard, restart, and numeric-boundary tests under the frozen exact representation and tie policy.
Bad: Partial matrices, floating-point drift, completion-order dependence, undocumented tolerances, or post-result tuning changes the mixture or response target.
Stakes: Critical
Source: Phase-265 D-11–D-13 and the committed synthetic numerical-spike policy.

Dimension: Response and counter coverage
Good: Each round targets the frozen meta-distribution and named strongest/vulnerable pure policies; accepted counters create the declared next snapshot and failed/weak attempts remain charged.
Bad: Only the average or easiest target is attacked, a late counter is omitted, or retries and runtime failures are hidden.
Stakes: High
Source: Phase-265 D-05, D-14, D-15, D-20; PSRO/double-oracle research.

Dimension: Robust pure versus mixture
Good: A finalist passes mixture, pure worst-case, accepted-counter, independent-probe, invariance, legality, privacy, runtime, and diversity gates; otherwise report “no robust pure finalist found.”
Bad: Aggregate win rate or mixture strength is treated as proof of a robust pure Strategy, Nash equilibrium, optimality, or permanent balance.
Stakes: Critical
Source: Phase-265 D-18–D-21 and activation-policy claim boundaries.

Dimension: Oracle-relative claims and legal information/privacy
Good: Reports name population, oracle families, budgets, solver, tuple, and roots, while keeping holdout, objectives, private memories, source, and formation artifacts out of unauthorized outputs.
Bad: Bounded oracle evidence is described as universal or production truth, or private information crosses the phase boundary/public replay.
Stakes: Critical
Source: Phase-265 D-20–D-21, AGENTS.md, and milestone activation policy.

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

1. Sparse or contaminated cells are coerced into scores, creating a flattering meta-game and invalid solver input.
2. Correlated or semantically duplicated Strategies/arenas inflate apparent diversity and response coverage.
3. Ordering, floating-point boundaries, worker scheduling, or post-result tuning changes a supposedly deterministic solver/reduction.
4. A successful counter or pure-policy worst case is omitted, or an oracle-relative mixture result is overstated as universal/production evidence.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

None identified for this private, offline research deployment. Constraints are project policies: immutable current-rules semantics, supervised hostile-source execution, fail-closed evidence admission, private-by-default artifacts, and no production certification or new legal claim.

### Core Imports
```ts
import {
  admitFrozenModelBundle,
  assertModelSourceClosure,
  emitModelFactoryPacket,
} from "@cowards/strategy-oracle-model"
```

### Entry Point Pattern
```ts
export function admitOfflineModelBundle(input: unknown) {
  // Unknown frozen data is canonicalised, bounded, validated, and deep-frozen.
  const bundle = admitFrozenModelBundle(input)
  // Source is checked for syntax, closure, denied capabilities, and runtime ABI;
  // this does not execute it or call a model.
  assertModelSourceClosure(bundle.response.source)
  return bundle
}

// After a separately frozen ModelFactoryRequest exists, use
// emitModelFactoryPacket(bundle, request). The returned packet is validated by
// FactoryOraclePacketSchema.parse and enters the existing supervised bridge.
```

### Key Abstractions
| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| Frozen model bundle | Content-addressed provider/request/response/source/accounting record | Admit external output before any league work |
| Canonical boundary | `admitFrozenModelBundle` plus exact root/provenance checks | Every unknown bundle or correction |
| Native source closure | `assertModelSourceClosure` validates TypeScript source and denied capabilities | Before packet emission; never direct `eval`/`new Function` |
| Factory packet | `emitModelFactoryPacket` produces a rooted private oracle packet | Convert an admitted bundle to the existing lab contract |
| Supervised runtime bridge | Existing runtime boundary owns hostile source execution and dispositions | Run Matches after admission, with failures retained |

### Common Pitfalls
1. Calling a provider from the league runner breaks replay identity, offline determinism, and charged budget accounting; consume frozen bundles only.
2. Treating `readonly` or a TypeScript cast as runtime validation admits forged roots or mutable evidence; use canonical admission and deep freezing.
3. Executing admitted source in the coordinator (including `eval`, `new Function`, or dynamic import) bypasses the hostile-source boundary; use the supervised bridge.
4. Keying cells by arena display ID or completion order double-counts semantic geometry and changes roots; use immutable candidate identity and `semanticGeometryHash`.
5. Selecting a solver before the numerical spike creates an unfrozen algorithm; the exact `bigint` solver and API remain proposed until golden/invariance vectors pass.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**
No provider, model, temperature, or budget is allocated yet. A separately frozen
offline producer must record provider/model identity, settings root, prompt/context
roots, token limit, actual usage, elapsed time, and response bytes. Missing or
drifting identity is a charged block, never substitution.

**Core Pattern:**
Admit one immutable bundle, verify its derived roots and native TypeScript lane,
validate source closure, then emit a factory packet and hand it to the existing
supervised runtime. The league coordinator may enumerate canonical cells and reduce
complete snapshots, but may not copy `MATCH_KERNEL` or execute source. The exact
solver interface below is proposed pending the numerical spike:

```ts
// Proposed API: freeze only after the synthetic spike and golden vectors.
export function solveSnapshot(snapshot: CompletePayoffSnapshot): SolverOutput {
  // Use exact rational weights backed by bigint; canonicalise entrant ordering,
  // tie-breaks, and output bytes before deriving the solver root.
  return solveWithFrozenBigintPolicy(snapshot)
}
```

**Tool Use:**
No live tools, provider SDK, hosted tracing, network, or semantic cache. Use the
existing private JSON traces, Zod-style/canonical contracts, `MATCH_KERNEL`, and
Vitest. Every retry, invalid output, rejection, duplicate, weak response, runtime
failure, and unused allocation remains a charged ledger event.

**State Management:**
Persist immutable content-addressed roots for population, pair/condition/arena
cells, snapshots, solver rounds, red-team allocations, attempts, portfolio, and
reports. Never mutate or resolve `latest`; corrections and retries create new roots.
Reduce only exact complete coverage, with canonical ordering independent of workers,
shards, restarts, and completion order.

**Context Window Strategy:**
The runner has no context window. Before bundling, the producer must bound prompt and
context bytes, set an explicit token limit, and record included/excluded context and
transformation roots. Never include holdout, objective payloads, private memories,
evaluator state, or formation artifacts.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

> Written by `gsd-ai-researcher`. Cross-cutting patterns every developer building AI systems needs — independent of framework choice.

### Structured Outputs with Zod (Pydantic N/A)

Pydantic is N/A: this repository is TypeScript-only and must not add a Python
dependency or a provider SDK. The canonical lab contracts are existing custom
Zod-style/canonical schemas; use Zod at any new unknown-data edge, then derive and
freeze a content root.

```ts
import { z } from "zod"

const FrozenBundleEnvelope = z.object({
  schemaVersion: z.enum(["frozen-model-bundle-v1", "frozen-model-bundle-v2"]),
  privacy: z.literal("private_offline"),
  root: z.string().regex(/^sha256:[0-9a-f]{64}$/u),
  source: z.object({ root: z.string(), byteLength: z.number().int().positive() }),
}).strict()

export function parseEnvelope(input: unknown) {
  return FrozenBundleEnvelope.parse(input) // canonical admission remains authoritative
}
```

For the full bundle, call the actual `admitFrozenModelBundle`; do not replace it
with a cast or partial schema. Parse/validation failure is one charged invalid
attempt. Retry only when the frozen allocation explicitly permits it (at most the
declared count); log error code, attempt root, input root, and resource accounting.
Surface the failure after the allocation is exhausted or on identity/provenance drift.

### Async-First Design

The league and canonical contracts are synchronous and deterministic; only an outer
file/worker adapter may be async. Do not call `asyncio.run()` (or introduce an event
loop) in this TypeScript lane, and do not turn async scheduling into solver state.
Await a complete frozen response before admission/hashing. Stream only for an external
producer's UX; await for structured validation, root derivation, and ledger commit.

### Prompt Engineering Discipline

Keep producer system instructions separate from task/context bytes and hash each
version. Few-shot examples must be fixed in the frozen request; dynamic retrieval is
not admissible. Set an explicit token limit in the bundle and reject missing,
unbounded, or unaccounted output. Prompts must not disclose holdout, objectives,
private memories, evaluator state, or formation artifacts.

### Context Window Management

This is an offline source-producing workflow, not RAG or conversation memory. Bound
context before the external call; if truncation or summarisation is necessary, record
the input, output, policy/version, and excluded-context roots. The runner receives no
hidden context and cannot repair an over-window bundle.

### Cost and Latency Budget

Exact per-call pricing is intentionally unallocated for Phase 265. Every attempt must
carry provider-reported input/output tokens, explicit token limit, elapsed time, and
resource root; unavailable accounting blocks rather than becoming zero. Exact-request
hash caching may reuse an immutable response only if the future frozen allocation explicitly permits it and retains its charge/provenance. Semantic caching is out of scope unless
its key and collision policy is separately frozen. Cheaper models for classification
or summarisation are allowed only in a future frozen producer plan, with each sub-call
and retry charged to the same allocation.

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Complete-cell admission | Missing, duplicate, conflicting, invalid, or system-failed identity; semantic alias collision | Block reduction and emit a charged process-failure disposition |
| Source/runtime boundary | Source closure, capability, schema, resource, or supervised-runtime check fails | Block execution; retain charged attempt and error root |
| Privacy/claim projection | Attempt to write source, memory, objective, holdout, formation, or deployment/public fields; unqualified oracle claim | Block publication and preserve private failure evidence |
| Frozen policy/identity | Allocation, target, threshold, solver manifest, or root differs from the frozen input | Stop the run; do not substitute, retry free of charge, or select stale `latest` |

### Offline (Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Matrix completeness and failure-disposition rate | Every source/injected run; future empirical snapshots only when authorized | Investigate and block any reduction if completeness is not exact; never coerce failures into scores |
| Solver/replay byte identity across nine probes | Every synthetic solver change and every retained snapshot/restart layout | Stop and reject the solver/snapshot until canonical roots and bytes match |
| Response coverage, counter re-entry, and charged-ledger reconciliation | Batch over each frozen response round and red-team allocation | Mark process invalid on omitted targets, missing charges, or absent next-round branch |
| Fingerprint novelty and diversity-gate evidence | Batch over each admitted candidate/portfolio snapshot | Reject clone/correlation inflation; preserve no-finalist outcome when gates fail |
| Pure robustness and claim/privacy boundary | Batch report audit before any finalist disposition | Emit `no_robust_pure_finalist_found` or process failure; quarantine leaked/overclaimed report |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** Existing private, offline, content-addressed JSON trace/shard/ledger
roots. This is not production monitoring and is not a deployable tracing service.
Every Match/cell, solver round, response attempt, retry, invalid/rejected/weak/system
failure/unfilled/unused event, and report stores its input/output/provenance roots,
resource accounting, and privacy class. No semantic cache or live provider call.

**Key Metrics to Track:** exact expected-versus-admitted cell count; disposition
counts and charged-ledger reconciliation; solver/replay byte-identity status;
response/independent-probe/fresh-red-team gate values under the frozen policy;
fingerprint diversity and robust-pure/no-finalist disposition; privacy/claim-boundary
violations. Metrics are private offline evidence, not product KPIs.

**Alert Thresholds:** fail closed on any missing/duplicate/conflicting/system-failed
cell, root/provenance mismatch, non-byte-identical invariance result, unauthorized
channel or field, omitted charged event, stale-target re-entry, or unqualified claim.
Apply the frozen policy gates exactly (`>55%` response, `>60%` independent probe,
`<60%` fresh red-team, minimum inventory/iteration gates); do not invent a pure
numeric floor or soften a gate. A process-valid failure is reported honestly.

**Smart Sampling Strategy:** There is no deployment/user stream. For private offline
review, retain all integrity failures and all charged non-success outcomes, then
sample traces with sparse-cell risk, duplicate/conflicting identity, solver tie or
boundary behavior, counter/re-entry, clone/novelty disagreement, privacy/claim
violations, or disagreement between code projections. Human/external sampling is
zero-authorized until a Phase-265 allocation explicitly binds opportunity, effort,
participant/provenance handling, and retry/burn rules; no fabricated labels or
Phase-264 zero waiver is permitted.
