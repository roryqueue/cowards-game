## Summary

The existing Phase 265 path has two independent 120-second provider clocks. The allocation’s `operations.perMatchMilliseconds` is validated against 120,000 ms and the currently approved prospective policy fixes it at 120,000. `createFactorySupervisedRuntime` admits at most 120,000 ms without an authority object (or 240,000 for the unrelated, single-use diagnostic-v4 grant); its timer includes nested runtime/container construction and checks both before and after each invoke. `createPlannerSupervisedRuntime` independently defaults to 120,000 ms and checks its own clock at invocation. Durable invocation retention occurs before control returns to the Match, so it consumes the absolute elapsed lifetime as well. These facts are corroborated by the v9 result and the lifetime accounting note.
