## Overview

v1.38 builds a private, content-addressed research control plane beside Coward's Game without creating a second engine, production runtime, public league feature, or rules version. It admits the exact v1.37 foundation, freezes measurement and holdout custody before search, proves a legal planner and deterministic runner, builds an immutable factory with independent response channels, completes and freezes a serious current-rules league, and only then materializes the three lab-only formation arms. The arms retrain separately under equal compute, freeze before one common sealed evaluation, and yield an honest causal pass or rejection while only eligible pre-formation current-rules finalists may enter ordinary product certification. Independent reproduction, a non-circular pre-tag audit, an exact archive commit, an annotated tag, and a read-only external post-tag attestation close the milestone.

## Binding Delivery Rules

- Authoritative work stops if the v1.37 audit, archive commit, annotated tag, post-tag attestation, or selected semantic/runtime authority is missing, stale, incompatible, or drifting. v1.38 does not repair predecessor defects.
- Phases 262-265 may precommit profile-neutral protocol and classifier fixtures, but no formation namespace, executable state, manifest, candidate, prompt, cache, trace, replay, or result may exist before Phase 266 emits a valid current-league freeze root.
- All three formation arms, including the current-edge control, produce private lab evidence only. None may become canonical registration, persistence, Chronicle, counted or exhibition scheduling, standings, product replay, or public/default output.
- Only exact pre-formation current-league finalist source hashes from the frozen eligible list may cross the current-only promotion bridge. Current-edge formation-control candidates are ineligible alongside inward and bracket artifacts.
- Cycle-cap, MOVE/reversal, Backstab geometry or timing, arena, runtime, product/public, and combined-rule changes remain outside v1.38.
- Contained spikes resolve research uncertainty before final architecture, but no spike may relax frozen rules, runtime, privacy, custody, production-reachability, or evidence-integrity boundaries.
- A process-valid current-rules empirical failure, no robust pure finalist, or bracket rejection is an honest result. Process, provenance, completeness, contamination, reproducibility, or integrity failure blocks successful audit/archive/tag closure.
