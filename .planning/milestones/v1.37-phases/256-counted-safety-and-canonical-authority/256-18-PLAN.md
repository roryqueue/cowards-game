<objective>
Connect verified append-only evidence, authenticated certificate status, and lane controls to the signed installed authority bundle consumed by scheduling, runtime-service, and Go.

Purpose: D-01 through D-04 require executable authority, not certificate-shaped rows or documentation. The existing bundle contract needs a production control plane that serializes exact verified records, signs outside the database, records provenance, and never replaces last-good authority on failure.
Output: Migration 0013, authenticated lane/revocation/supersession imports, deterministic locked publisher, external-key CLI, durable publication ledger, and atomic-install failure proof.
</objective>

## Artifacts this phase produces

- Separate append-only authenticated lane-control, certificate-revocation, certificate-supersession, and authority-publication tables with database mutation-rejection triggers.
- One serialized deterministic publisher that consumes only verified closed evidence and records every selected source ID/hash.
- An external-Ed25519-key CLI and same-directory fsync/rename installer with recoverable prepared/installed/failed event history.
